A simple Android app with decent UI to get you updated with the current statistics of COVID-19 of Bangladesh along with worldwide stats.

## Features
* Bangladesh COVID-19 Statistics
* Worwide Statistics
* COVID-19 Hospitals and Emergency numbers of Bangladesh
* Myths, Handwash details procedures, Infos, FAQ and so on
* Donation
